Jiotty Photos Uploader privacy policy

Jiotty Photos Uploader is a desktop application that uploads photos and videos from your computer to your Google Photos library. It runs entirely on your computer: there is no Jiotty server, and the developer never receives your data.

What the application accesses in your Google account

When you log in, you grant Jiotty Photos Uploader these permissions, and it uses them only as described:

Permission What Jiotty Photos Uploader does with it
Add to your Google Photos library
photoslibrary.appendonly		 Uploads the photos and videos you select, and creates albums named after your folders.
View and manage photos, videos and albums created by this app
photoslibrary.readonly.appcreateddata, photoslibrary.edit.appcreateddata		 Lists the albums it created earlier, so that it reuses them instead of creating duplicates, and adds uploaded items to them. It cannot see anything else in your library.
View and manage its own configuration data in your Google Drive
drive.appdata		 Reads how much of your Google storage is used and how much is left, to show it while uploading. To get up-to-date figures it creates and immediately deletes an empty file in a hidden folder that only this app can see. It cannot see your Drive files.

What is stored, and where

Everything is stored on your computer only, in the .jiottyphotosuploader folder in your home folder:

  • the login token that lets the application access your Google account on your behalf, in a folder only your user account can read. Logging out in the application's Preferences deletes it, and you can revoke the application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions;
  • which files have already been uploaded, so that an interrupted upload can resume;
  • your settings, and log files that contain the names of the files and folders processed.

None of this is sent anywhere. Apart from Google's services, the only network request the application makes is to GitHub, to check whether a newer version has been released; that request contains no information about you.

Your photos and videos are uploaded to your own Google Photos library and are not copied anywhere else.

Use of Google user data

Jiotty Photos Uploader's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Jiotty Photos Uploader does not sell, share or transfer Google user data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. No person reads your data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: raise an issue at github.com/ylexus/jiotty-photos-uploader/issues.

Effective 7 November 2026.
