Jiotty Photos Uploader

A free, open-source desktop app for Windows and macOS that uploads folders of photos and videos to your Google Photos library, creating an album for each folder.

What it does

  • Scans a folder and all its subfolders for photos and videos and uploads them to your own Google Photos library.
  • Creates albums named after your folders and puts each photo in the album for its folder.
  • Remembers what it has uploaded, so an interrupted upload resumes where it left off.

It runs entirely on your computer and talks only to Google; there is no Jiotty server. See the privacy policy for exactly what it accesses in your Google account and why.
